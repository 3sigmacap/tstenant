TsTenantby 3 Sigma Solutions

Rent, lease and repairs in one place

TsTenant is the tenant app for property managers who keep their books in TSBooks.

  • Pay rent from a saved bank account or card, or set up autopay once and let rent pay itself each month.
  • Ask for repairs with photos from the camera, and follow each request from the first reply to the finished job.
  • Message the property manager in one thread, and get a notification when an answer arrives.
  • See the lease: rent, deposit, dates, people on the lease and everything charged and paid.
  • Stay notified when rent is due, a visit is scheduled or a document is ready to sign.

Getting in

A property manager invites each tenant by email. The invitation link signs the tenant in the first time; after that, signing in takes the email address and a six-digit code.

Help

Questions about rent, a lease or a repair go to the property manager, from the Messages tab. Questions about the app itself: support@3sigma.solutions.
